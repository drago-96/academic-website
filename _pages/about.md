I'm Riccardo, originally from Turin, Italy, and currently living in Saarbrücken, Germany.

### Research

My research interests are quite broad, but they mostly revolve around cryptographic protocols, their security, and their practical applications.

Throughout my PhD, I've worked on cryptographic group actions, in particular CSIDH and isogeny-based cryptography. Given my mathematical background, I find the rich structure of isogenies fascinating, as well as the challenge of turning these mathematical objects into secure and efficient cryptographic constructions.

I'm also interested in advanced cryptographic primitives, including blind signatures, adaptor signatures, and fully homomorphic encryption (FHE). Much of my work focuses on developing appropriate security models and designing protocols with rigorous security guarantees.

More recently, I've also been working on cryptanalysis, from attacks against FHE scheme switching with [Giacomo](https://giacomosantato.github.io/personal-website/) to vulnerabilities in TLS-RSA key exchange. My favorite problems are those that bring together theoretical security analysis and practical attacks or implementations.

### Education

<!-- #### University -->

I studied Mathematics at the [University of Pisa](https://www.unipi.it/en/), where I obtained my Bachelor's degree in 2018 and Master's degree in 2021. During the same period, I attended the _Corso Ordinario_ in Mathematics at [Scuola Normale Superiore](https://www.sns.it/en).

### CTFs

I'm also a passionate CTF player! I enjoy playing with the Italian teams **pwnthem0le** and **about:blankets** whenever I have a free weekend.

Some highlights include winning the 2020 national [CyberChallenge.IT](https://cyberchallenge.it/) competition with the University of Pisa team, and representing Italy at the European Cyber Security Challenge ([ECSC](https://ecsc.eu)) in Prague in 2021, where we earned a bronze medal.

I've also spent a lot of time solving challenges on [CryptoHack](https://cryptohack.org/), and really enjoy its community!

Besides competing, I also enjoy contributing to the CTF community. I help organize [m0leCon](https://m0lecon.it/), a cybersecurity conference and CTF in my hometown of Turin, and have authored challenges for international competitions such as ICC and ECSC.
I've also worked with CINI on CyberChallenge.IT and [OliCyber](https://olicyber.it/en), designing CTF challenges and teaching cybersecurity courses to students.

You can find more details about these contributions on my [Activities]({{ '/activities/' | relative_url }}) page.
